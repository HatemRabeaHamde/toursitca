iconic dishes.

Tagine

Slow-cooked stew of lamb, chicken or vegetables with aromatic spices in a conical clay pot.

Couscous

The national dish — steamed semolina with seven vegetables, traditionally eaten on Fridays.

Harira

A rich, hearty tomato & lentil soup — the traditional soup to break the Ramadan fast.

Mint Tea

Moroccan whisky — the ritual of pouring from height, shared with every guest as a welcome.

Got questions?

Everything you need
to know.

How do I book an experience on TourstiCa?

+
Simply search for your destination and preferred activity, choose a date and number of travelers, then pay securely online. You'll receive a confirmation email with your booking details and guide's contact info within minutes.

What is your cancellation policy?

+
Most experiences offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start time. Some premium or small-group experiences may have a 48-hour or 72-hour policy — this is clearly displayed on each experience page before you book.

Are the guides vetted and certified?

+
Yes. Every guide and agency on TourstiCa is manually reviewed and must hold the relevant local tourism certifications. We verify identity, insurance, and safety standards before any listing goes live. Guides also maintain a minimum 4.5-star rating to stay active.

What currencies and payment methods are accepted?

+
You can pay in MAD, EUR, USD, or GBP. We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are SSL-encrypted and PCI-DSS compliant. No hidden fees — the price you see is the price you pay.

Can I book a private or custom experience?

+
Absolutely. Many experiences on TourstiCa offer a "Private" option at checkout, which gives you an exclusive booking with the guide for just your group. For fully custom itineraries — especially for groups of 6 or more — you can use our "Request a quote" form.

Is pickup included in the experience price?

+
Pickup availability varies by experience. Experiences that include hotel pickup display a 🚐 pickup badge on the listing card and confirm the pickup point during checkout. If pickup is not included, meeting-point details are clearly provided in your booking confirmation.
